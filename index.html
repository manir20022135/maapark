App Features

Everything you need for a safe and informed pregnancy journey.

Pregnancy Tracker

Track your week-by-week pregnancy progress with baby's size, development, and milestones. Automatically calculates your week, providing daily tips, growth charts, and ultrasound reminders.

Prenatal Yoga

Pregnancy-safe yoga routines and videos for physical and mental wellness. Week-wise guided video sessions (10-30 min) from beginner to advanced, with animated pose instructions.

Child Care Guide

A complete guide from newborn to toddler, covering feeding, sleeping, and milestones. Features age-based articles, videos, and checklists with offline download options.

Self Care

Daily self-care tips to prevent motherhood burnout. Includes a mood tracker, meditation audios, journaling prompts, and weekly self-assessment quizzes.

Consult an Expert

100% IN-APP

Book secure, private video consultations powered by ZEGOCLOUD API. Koi extra app nahi chahiye — video call 100% MaaCare ke andar hi hota hai without any third-party redirects.

Family Planning

Tools and WHO-guided information to help you plan your family's future. Learn about fertility awareness, postpartum contraception options, and infertility support.

Contraception Guide

A comprehensive guide to various modern contraceptive methods. Explore hormonal, barrier, IUD, and permanent methods with effectiveness, pros, and cons.

Vaccinations Tracker

A tracker for baby and mom's vaccination schedules. Sets up a calendar based on WHO/Indian guidelines with reminders and nearby clinic information.

Nutrition Guide

Balanced diet plans and recipes for pregnancy and postpartum periods. Includes calorie calculator, grocery lists, easy Indian recipes, and allergy filters.

Symptom Checker

A quick diagnosis tool for common pregnancy symptoms. Uses symptom selector to indicate risk level with 'See Doctor?' alerts and follow-up chatbot.

AI Companion

A 24/7 smart chatbot that answers questions like 'How many baby kicks are normal?' Understands natural language queries and provides personalized responses.

Parents Park

A safe, moderated forum for moms to share experiences. Post anonymously, filter by pregnancy week, react with likes, and chat live with other mamas.

Period Dashboard

A complete menstrual cycle tracker for girls — predict cycles, track ovulation, log moods, view period history, and access cycle-specific nutrition guides.

Child Growth Tracker

Track your baby's height, weight, and development milestones against WHO growth standards. Interactive charts (fl_chart) with age-wise health benchmarks.

Gamification & Rewards

Earn points for daily logins (+15), mood check-ins (+5), tasks (+10), and community posts. Unlock badge tiers from New Mama to Legend Mama 🏆

Privacy Policy for MaaCare

At Maa Park, your trust means everything. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data securely, fully complying with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR guidelines.

Last Updated: April 23, 2026 · End-to-End Encrypted

1. Data We Collect

  • Personal: Name, age, email, phone.
  • Health: Pregnancy stage, mood, health vitals.
  • Baby Care: Growth stats, nutrition & vaccines.
  • UGC: Anonymous community forum posts.

2. How We Use Data

  • Personalize AI chat & doctor recommendations.
  • Secure in-app doctor video consultations.
  • Daily mood, vaccination & medication alerts.
  • Aggregated clinical metrics to improve care.

3. Data Security & Storage

  • Encrypted MongoDB Atlas cloud database on AWS.
  • ZEGOCLOUD end-to-end encrypted video.
  • PCI-DSS compliant payments via Razorpay.
  • Strict HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

4. Your Rights (DPDP & GDPR)

  • Access & Portability: Export health history.
  • Correction: Update medical profile anytime.
  • Erasure: Permanent one-click account deletion.
  • Anonymity: 100% anonymous peer support forums.

5. Trusted Third Parties

  • ZEGOCLOUD & Razorpay: Video & Payments.
  • AWS & MongoDB Atlas: Cloud & DB Storage.
  • OneSignal: Critical Vaccination Reminders.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers: We never sell data.

6. Child Data Protection

MaaCare accounts are intended for adult mothers & guardians (18+). Baby health logs are maintained strictly within the guardian's encrypted account for paediatric care.
