1. Official Terms & Conditions (Governing Campaign & 1% Equity Award)

1.1 Eligibility & Jurisdiction

The MaaPark Brand Ambassador & App Promotion Hunt 2026–2027 ("Campaign") is organized by MaaPark Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Company"). Participation is open to individuals who are:

  • Citizens or legal permanent residents of the Republic of India;
  • Aged eighteen (18) years or older at the time of registration;
  • Enrolled in or graduated from recognized colleges, universities, healthcare institutions, or working professionals with interest in digital health advocacy.

1.2 The 1% Equity Award & 3-Year Vesting Schedule

The selected Brand Ambassador will be awarded 1% (one percent) equity in MaaPark Technologies (or designated operating entity), structured strictly under the following mandatory terms:

  • Vesting Schedule: The 1% equity will vest linearly over a period of three (3) years with annual milestone reviews, following execution of a formal Advisor / Ambassador Stock Agreement.
  • Deliverables & Scope of Role: The Ambassador will represent MaaPark at regional health seminars, college campuses, maternal health awareness drives, social media broadcasts, and community outreach campaigns for the MaaPark Mobile App.
  • No Guaranteed Employment: Registration, participation, and completion of the assessment evaluation do not create any employer-employee relationship or guarantee of employment.
Selection Transparency: Final selection of the Brand Ambassador is based on the composite evaluation of: (1) Step 3 Vision & Background essays, (2) Step 4 40-MCQ Assessment Score, and (3) a short 10-minute online video interview with the Top 10 finalists. The decision of the MaaPark Selection Committee is final, conclusive, and legally binding.

1.3 Campaign Milestones

  • Campaign Run: Active for 3 months until late December 2026.
  • Test Score Display: Displays strictly raw score (e.g. "Score: 34/40") immediately after completing Step 4. No pass/fail or ranking decision is final until conclusion of video interviews.
  • Grand Winner Announcement: 1 January 2027 on maapark.in.
  • E-Certificate Availability: Official certificates remain locked until 10 January 2027.

2. Privacy Policy (DPDP Act 2023 & IT Act 2000 Compliance)

MaaPark Technologies respects and protects your fundamental right to privacy in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2.1 Data We Collect

  • Identity Data: Full legal name, unique candidate ID (MP-AMB-2026-XXXX).
  • Contact Information: Mobile telephone number (authenticated via OTP), verified email address, city, and state of residence.
  • Profile & Professional Data: Educational background, college or institution name, current professional role, social media profile links (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).
  • Qualitative Assessment Insights: Candidate bio, app promotion strategy, maternal healthcare perspectives, and 40-MCQ assessment answers.
  • Transaction Records: Razorpay / gateway payment order ID, payment transaction ID, timestamp, and generated promotional activation codes. (MaaPark does never store credit/debit card numbers or bank passwords).

2.2 Purpose & Use of Data

Your personal data is strictly processed to:

  • Authenticate your candidate registration and dispatch security verification OTPs;
  • Process assessment evaluations and compile objective scoring dossiers for selection;
  • Provision and deliver your 1-Year Free Premium subscription code for the MaaPark Mobile App;
  • Generate and issue cryptographically verifiable PDF advocacy certificates;
  • Contact Top 10 shortlisted finalists for scheduling video interviews.
Strict Non-Disclosure Commitment: MaaPark Technologies does never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or health data to commercial ad brokers, insurance companies, or unauthorized third parties.

3. Fee Structure & Payment / Refund Policy (Razorpay Compliant)

3.1 Fee Breakdown (₹499 Non-Refundable Evaluation Fee)

Every registered candidate contributes an all-inclusive evaluation and service fee of ₹499 (Rupees Four Hundred and Ninety-Nine only). This fee covers:

  • Candidate Evaluation & Assessment Processing: Secure test room provisioning, anti-cheating tab monitoring, and panel dossier review;
  • 1-Year Free Premium Access to the MaaPark Mobile App: Complete access to priority gynecologist tele-consultations, period tracking, and maternal tracking tools (valued at ₹1,999, included at zero additional cost);
  • Verifiable Certified Advocacy Credential: Official cryptographic PDF certificate with verification QR code.

3.2 Non-Refundable Nature

Mandatory Notice: Payment of the ₹499 fee is strictly non-refundable once processed. Because candidate enrollment immediately grants server-side test provisioning, generates a unique candidate credential ID, and delivers an instant 1-Year Free Premium App promo code, no refund requests will be entertained on grounds of change of mind, failure to take the exam, or non-selection for the equity award.

3.3 Failed Transactions & Duplicate Deductions

In the event of a technical disruption where a candidate's bank account is debited multiple times for a single registration:

  • The excess duplicate deduction will be automatically refunded by our payment gateway (Razorpay / Cashfree) back to the originating bank account or card within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Candidates may notify our billing desk at support@maapark.in with the transaction reference number (UTR) for prompt resolution.

4. Assessment Code of Conduct & Anti-Cheating Policy

MaaPark upholds the highest standards of medical and academic ethics. The 25-minute online assessment is administered under strict algorithmic oversight:

  • Single Active Browser Tab Lock: Candidates must keep the test tab active and focused. Switching tabs, minimizing windows, or navigating away is detected and logged by our client-server audit engine. Excessive tab switching may result in score penalization or profile invalidation.
  • 25-Minute Hard Time Limit: Assessment timer is synced with server timestamps. Upon expiration of the 25:00 countdown, the exam room automatically finalizes and logs all current responses.
  • Plagiarism & Ghostwriting Prohibited: Qualitative responses in Step 3 must be the original creative work of the candidate. Use of unauthorized automated AI scraping bots to spam responses is strictly grounds for immediate disqualification.

5. E-Certificate Issuance & Lock Policy

Official credentials for the designation of "Certified Maternal & Women’s Health Advocate" are governed by the following schedule:

  • Scheduled Release Date: Certificates will remain locked and will unlock for direct download starting on 10 January 2027.
  • Verification & Validity: Each certificate contains an indelible SHA-256 digital verification hash, candidate roll number, and live online QR verification link hosted on maapark.in.
